## Platform

web

## Users

- Prospective clients, startup founders, and engineering recruiters evaluating technical depth, full-stack competence, and product design execution.
- Fellow software engineers and the Indonesian developer community reading candid technical essays, tutorials, and reflections on programming.

## Product Purpose

- Serve as the flagship portfolio and personal platform for Bima (BimaDev), showcasing full-stack web applications, AI tooling, and technical writing.
- Success means establishing immediate credibility as a modern FullStack Developer with strong UI craft, leading to inbound project inquiries, freelance opportunities, or engineering roles.

## Positioning

- A FullStack Developer from Indonesia who bridges robust full-stack systems (Next.js, Supabase, APIs) with AI integration and meticulous UI/UX design engineering (Once UI, custom design tokens, micro-interactions).

## Operating Context

- Browsed across desktop monitors and mobile devices by technical recruiters, collaborators, and clients reviewing project breakdowns, live demos, and blog essays.
- Contact pathways initiated directly via WhatsApp, Instagram, or direct email (`contact@bimadev.online`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Once UI component architecture, SCSS modules, Framer Motion, and MDX-powered blog/project systems.
- Password-protected project showcase routes supported via internal authentication API.
- Maintain Once UI design system conventions, CSS tokens, and existing `react-icons` registry without introducing arbitrary third-party UI dependencies.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Bima / BimaDev
- Role: FullStack Developer
- Location: Asia/Pontianak (Indonesia)
- Voice & Tone: Authentic, reflective, craft-driven, pragmatic, and approachable.
- Primary Channels: Direct Email, WhatsApp, Instagram.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof Over Promises**: Lead with tangible, working projects, real code, and actual case studies rather than unsubstantiated claims.
2. **Design-Engineered Craft**: Deliver interface precision, fluid motion, crisp typography, and responsive ergonomics as a first-class engineering discipline.
3. **Frictionless Engagement**: Keep navigation intuitive and provide immediate routes to project details, blog insights, and direct communication.
4. **Authentic Voice**: Reflect real developer perspectives, personal growth, and practical engineering trade-offs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Dark-themed high-contrast readability, semantic landmarks, full keyboard navigation support, responsive reflow across viewports, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
